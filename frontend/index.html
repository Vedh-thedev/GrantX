How It Works

End-to-End Grant Workflow

From idea to funded project in a fully structured, transparent process.

1
Student Submits Proposal
Student completes a 5-step wizard covering team details, innovation description, novelty assessment, patent potential, and detailed budget breakdown.
2
R&D Cell Initial Processing
The R&D Committee reviews the submission and assigns exactly two independent faculty evaluators with deadlines.
3
Independent Faculty Evaluation
Both evaluators independently score the proposal on Novelty (40%), Feasibility (30%), and Commercial Impact (30%), providing detailed comments and recommendations.
4
Automatic Score Aggregation
The system automatically calculates each evaluator's weighted score and aggregates both scores into a final score out of 10.
5
R&D Grant Decision
The committee reviews all evaluation data and makes a formal decision: Approve, Reject, or Request Revision with documented rationale.
6
Grant Sanction & Milestone Tracking
Approved projects receive sanctioned funding and milestone tracking. Students can monitor progress through their dashboard in real-time.
Features

Built for Every Stakeholder

Purpose-built tools for students, evaluators, and the R&D Committee.

📋
5-Step Submission Wizard
Guided multi-step form ensuring students provide all required information including team details, proposal content, novelty assessment, and budget.
👁️
Blinded Peer Review
Evaluators review independently. Neither evaluator can see the other's private review while evaluation is ongoing — ensuring unbiased assessment.
🧮
Automatic Score Calculation
Backend calculates weighted scores (Novelty 40%, Feasibility 30%, Commercial Impact 30%) and aggregates both evaluator scores automatically.
📊
Real-Time Tracking
Visual workflow tracker shows students exactly where their proposal is in the 16-stage lifecycle from DRAFT to COMPLETED.
🎯
Milestone Management
R&D Cell creates and manages project milestones for approved grants. Students can monitor completion progress visually.
🔔
Smart Notifications
Database-backed notifications keep all parties informed at every key workflow event — submission, assignment, evaluation, decisions, and milestones.
